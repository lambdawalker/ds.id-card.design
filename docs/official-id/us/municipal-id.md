# Municipal resident ID

[United States index](README.md) · `us.municipal-id`

- **Issuer** Participating city or local government
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify against specimen
- **Scope** IDNYC is one example

## Human-readable fields

Check holder name, portrait, city or program, card number, date of birth, address and validity dates where printed. Address disclosure and optional fields vary by issuer.

## Machine-readable data

Check for a local barcode or QR. Separate embedded fields from issuer verification results. IDNYC does not establish the payload for other municipalities.
