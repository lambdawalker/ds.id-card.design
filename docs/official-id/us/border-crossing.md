# Border Crossing Card (DSP-150 / BCC)

[United States index](README.md) · `us.border-crossing`

- **Issuer** Department of State
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify against specimen

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, date of birth, nationality, card number, visa class, issue and expiration dates against the DSP-150 edition.

## Machine-readable data

Inspect for a printed machine-readable zone and RFID by generation. The CBP privacy source describes the RFID lookup model, with payload for the specific BCC edition unverified here.
