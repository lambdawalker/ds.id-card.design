# Common Access Card (CAC)

[United States index](README.md) · `us.military-cac`

- **Issuer** Department of Defense
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify against specimen
- **Scope** Uniformed personnel and eligible civilian or contractor categories

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, affiliation, rank or pay grade where applicable, service or agency, expiration and identifiers on the relevant CAC edition.

## Machine-readable data

Smart chip supports identity and access functions. Verify certificate and attribute categories against the relevant edition or official profile. Treat certificate data and any printed barcode as separate channels.
