# Personal Identity Verification (PIV) card

[United States index](README.md) · `us.federal-piv`

- **Issuer** Issuing federal agency
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Standard reference, verify agency implementation
- **Scope** HSPD-12 federal personnel credential

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, agency, affiliation, expiration and any card serial or agency-specific markings.

## Machine-readable data

The smart chip can hold identity authentication certificates and a card or credential identifier. Agency access decisions and backend personnel data are separate. Verify optional attributes and edition requirements against the PIV profile.
