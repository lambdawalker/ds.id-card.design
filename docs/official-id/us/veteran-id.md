# Veteran ID Card (VIC)

[United States index](README.md) · `us.veteran-id`

- **Issuer** Department of Veterans Affairs
- **Typical format** digital
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify against specimen
- **Scope** Veteran status credential, distinct from VHIC

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, veteran status wording and issuing authority on the digital credential. Verify any service branch or card reference shown.

## Machine-readable data

No public payload established here. Check the digital artifact separately from historic physical cards and from the VHIC.
