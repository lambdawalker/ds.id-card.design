# Commercial learner’s permit (CLP)

[United States index](README.md) · `us.driver-commercial-permit`

- **Issuer** State or participating jurisdiction
- **Typical format** varies
- **Evidence** U.S. REAL ID baseline, confirm jurisdiction and edition

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, date of birth, address, credential number, issue date, expiry, sex marker and signature. Driver credentials may also show class, endorsements and restrictions. Other physical descriptors vary by jurisdiction. Record commercial class and restrictions or endorsements separately from the credential number.

## Machine-readable data

Confirm permit medium and code presence first. For REAL ID editions, the PDF417 barcode carries expiration date, name, issue date, date of birth, sex marker, address, unique card number, card design revision date, inventory control number and issuing jurisdiction. AAMVA editions can carry additional fields, including driving privileges and descriptors, subject to issuer adoption. Confirm actual fields for standard, limited-term, and temporary editions separately.
