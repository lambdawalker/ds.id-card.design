# Driver’s license

[United States index](README.md) · `us.driver-license`

- **Issuer** State, DC or territorial licensing authority
- **Typical format** varies
- **Evidence** U.S. REAL ID baseline, confirm jurisdiction and edition
- **Scope** Track standard, REAL ID and enhanced variants where offered

## Human-readable fields

Check name, portrait, date of birth, address, credential number, issue date, expiry, sex marker and signature. Driver credentials may also show class, endorsements and restrictions. Other physical descriptors vary by jurisdiction.

## Machine-readable data

For REAL ID editions, the PDF417 barcode carries expiration date, name, issue date, date of birth, sex marker, address, unique card number, card design revision date, inventory control number and issuing jurisdiction. AAMVA editions can carry additional fields, including driving privileges and descriptors, subject to issuer adoption. Confirm actual fields for standard, limited-term, and temporary editions separately.
