# Tarjeta de Visitante Trabajador Fronterizo

[Mexico index](README.md) · `mx.visitor-border-worker`

- **Issuer** INM
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Historical issuer specification, verify newer editions

## Human-readable fields

The circular lists name, nationality, country and date of birth, sex, portrait, immigration status, foreign-national identifier (NUE), expiration when applicable and CURP where required. The reverse includes signature, issuance place and date, migration document number, authority and renewal count where applicable. The circular provides a family-member indicator where relevant and excludes renewal count.

## Machine-readable data

The circular lists a reverse OCR zone. Field-level payload, format and any QR or chip on later editions require specimen verification.
