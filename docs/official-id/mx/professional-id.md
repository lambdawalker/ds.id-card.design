# Cédula profesional

[Mexico index](README.md) · `mx.professional-id`

- **Issuer** SEP / Dirección General de Profesiones
- **Typical format** varies
- **Evidence** Documented specimen
- **Scope** Record photo presence and medium by edition

## Human-readable fields

Check holder name, cédula number, CURP, professional title or degree, institution, and issuance details. Record photo and signature only where the edition displays them.

## Machine-readable data

The cited court decision documents a QR and separate barcodes for the cédula number and electronic credential identifier on the specimen reviewed. The court identifies name and professional license number as public code information in that case. Verify the mapping separately for other editions.
