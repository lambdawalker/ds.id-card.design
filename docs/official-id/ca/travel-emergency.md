# Emergency travel document

[Canada index](README.md) · `ca.travel-emergency`

- **Issuer** Canadian mission abroad
- **Typical format** document
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify against specimen

## Human-readable fields

Check names, date and place of birth, portrait, document number, issue and expiry dates, issuing authority, and travel restrictions or endorsements. Verify nationality or status labels on the applicable edition.

## Machine-readable data

Check the data page for an MRZ and chip by edition. Where an ICAO TD3 MRZ is confirmed, its field categories follow the passport-booklet standard. The source here does not establish a payload for this credential.
