# Photo health or services card

[Canada index](README.md) · `ca.health-services-id`

- **Issuer** Provincial or territorial health authority
- **Typical format** card
- **Evidence** Candidate fields, verify by program
- **Scope** Examples include BC Services Card and combined BC driver’s licence/services card. Photo and permitted use vary.

## Human-readable fields

Check name, member or health number, issuer, photo, date of birth, issue or expiry date and coverage class where visible. Separate any driver license fields on combined cards.

## Machine-readable data

Confirm technology and payload by program. A member identifier and a backend eligibility result are separate observations.
