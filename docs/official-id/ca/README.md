## Supporting documents

Birth certificate, citizenship certificate, Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR), permanent resident travel document (PRTD), Verification of Status, visitor record, work permit, study permit, visa, and non-photo health coverage card. [ca-cit](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/proof-citizenship/about.html), [ca-rpid](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/status-documents.html), [ca-border](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/travel-voyage/td-dv-eng.html)

## Issuer coverage

**Canada · 10 provinces and 3 territories.** Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Saskatchewan, Northwest Territories, Nunavut and Yukon. Check driver and learner products, non-driver cards, photo health and combined cards, and legacy enhanced credentials separately. FINTRAC links to licence issuers for all 13 jurisdictions and gives examples of non-driver IDs. [ca-fin](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/guidance-directives/client-clientele/guide11/11-eng)
